As a kid raised by internet forums, I was obsessed with customizing my signatures and &ldquo;about me&rdquo; sections on personal profiles using [BBCode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBCode) in data science with <abbr title="Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence">ML/AI</abbr> emphasis, but as a childhood dream of mine I wanted to design on the web. To that end, I moonlight as a [contractable](mailto:jason@enscribe.dev) design engineer —— I&rsquo;m currently at [OtterSec](https://osec.io) and [Hacktron](https://hacktron.ai). I am also pretty heavily involved in the cybersecurity sphere, where I write [capture-the-flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capture_the_flag_(cybersecurity)) security challenges and organize international competitions for [Project Sekai CTF](https://sekai.team) and the [Friendly Maltese Citizens](https://ctf.mt) (which is why the majority of my design work pertains to security companies). If you want to see more of what I do, check out my [/work](/work) page.

Outside of my professional life, I enjoy everything music: discovering new albums, attending concerts, playing classical & electric guitar, and even going to arcades to play rhythm games.
